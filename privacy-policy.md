# Privacy Policy — Luxe Living Studio

**Last updated:** October 12, 2026

## Overview
Luxe Living Studio is a personal project designed to help its owner prepare Pinterest content related to home decor and interior design.

## Information and Pinterest Access
If the application connects to Pinterest, it will request only the permissions necessary for its intended features. Pinterest authorization will be handled through Pinterest's official authorization process. The application will not ask users to provide their Pinterest password directly.

## Use of Information
Information accessed through an authorized connection will be used only for the application's content-management features, such as preparing Pin drafts and organizing content for review.

## Storage and Security
The project owner will take reasonable steps to protect any stored information and access tokens. Access tokens will not intentionally be published in the public source-code repository.

## Sharing
Personal information and authorization credentials will not be sold. Information will not be shared with third parties except where necessary to operate the application or comply with applicable law.

## Data Deletion
Users may request deletion of information held by the project owner by contacting the project owner through the contact method provided with the application.

## Contact
For privacy questions or data-deletion requests, contact the project owner through the contact method provided with the application.

## Updates
This policy may be updated when the application's features or data practices change.
